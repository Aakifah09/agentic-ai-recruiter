Job Title: AI Engineer - Agentic Systems

Northwind Analytics is building LLM-powered assistants for enterprise customers. You will join a small team that designs, ships and evaluates multi-agent workflows.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and build agentic AI workflows that orchestrate multiple LLM agents
- Develop retrieval augmented generation (RAG) pipelines over enterprise documents
- Write and iterate on prompts, and build evaluation harnesses to measure quality
- Expose AI services through FastAPI and deploy them with Docker
- Work with product owners and stakeholders to translate business requirements into AI solutions

Requirements:
- 3+ years of professional experience in software or machine learning engineering
- Strong Python skills
- Hands-on experience with LLMs and prompt engineering
- Experience building RAG systems and AI agents
- Experience with REST APIs (FastAPI or Flask)
- Docker and Git
- Solid SQL
- Experience with model evaluation and testing of ML systems
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field

Nice to have:
- LangChain or LlamaIndex
- Vector databases such as FAISS or pgvector
- AWS or Azure
- Kubernetes
- Streamlit for internal demos

What we offer:
- Hybrid working, learning budget, private health insurance
